## Full Description

Focus Flow is a productivity app designed to help you organize your tasks and work with greater focus using the Pomodoro technique. Everything works locally on your device, with no internet required and no account to create.

**Task Management**
Create, edit, and delete tasks. Mark them as complete and keep your list always up to date.

**Configurable Pomodoro Timer**
Work in focused sessions with short and long breaks. Configure the duration of each session and the number of cycles before the long break to match your workflow.

**Sessions Linked to Tasks**
Associate each Pomodoro session with a specific task to know exactly how much time you spend on each activity.

**Productivity Statistics**
View your metrics: completed sessions, total focus time, and distribution by task. See your daily activity and discover patterns in how you work.

**Interface in Spanish and English**
Use the app in the language you prefer. The change applies immediately from Settings.

**Visual Themes**
Choose from three color palettes — Warm, Nature, and Ocean — with light and dark mode. Customize the appearance to your liking.

**Completely Offline**
Your data never leaves your device. No personal information is collected, there are no ads, and no data is shared with third parties. When you uninstall the app, everything is deleted.

**Material 3 Design**
Modern and accessible interface following Material 3 design guidelines.
